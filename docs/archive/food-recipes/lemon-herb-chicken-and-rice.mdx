This one-pan sear pairs lemony, oregano-forward chicken with fluffy rice for a bright, savory meal.

## Ingredients
### Sear Chicken
- Chicken breast (boneless, skinless), 200 g
- Lemon juice, 1/2 lemon (freshly squeezed)
- Salt, 1/4 tsp
- Paprika, 1 tsp
- Garlic powder, 1 tsp
- Oregano flakes, 1 tsp
- Olive oil, 1 tsp (for searing)

### Lemon and herb rice sear
- Water, 2 cups (500 mL)
- Cooked rice, 160 g
- Onion, 1/2 medium (60 g), finely diced
- Garlic, 2 cloves, finely minced
- Lemon juice, 1/2 lemon (freshly squeezed)
- Oregano flakes, 1 1/2 tsp
- Paprika, 1 tsp
- Garlic powder, 1 tsp
- Fresh dill, finely chopped (optional, for garnish)

## Instructions
### 1. Sear the chicken
- Preheat a greased stainless steel pan or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat.
- Pat chicken thighs dry, then season with lemon juice, salt, paprika, garlic powder, and oregano.
- Sear for 8-10 minutes total, flipping halfway, until both sides are browned. Set aside.

### 2. Build the rice base
- In the same pan, add garlic and onions. Saute for 2-3 minutes until lightly browned.
- Add rinsed rice, seasonings, and lemon juice. Stir until the rice looks slightly dry and toasted.

### 3. Simmer and sear
- Lower heat to medium and add water. Stir once and let it come to a bubbly simmer for about 3 minutes.
- Nestle the seared chicken into the rice. Cover and cook for 10-15 minutes, until the liquid is mostly absorbed.

### 4. Rest and serve
- Remove from heat and let it sit, covered, for about 5 minutes so the rice steams and flavors meld.
- Garnish with fresh dill if using, then serve warm.

---

## Chef's Notes
- Keep the heat steady so the rice cooks evenly without scorching.
- If your pan runs hot, add a splash of water near the end and rest a little longer.
