STEP 1 — COOK THE KIDNEY BEANS (DAY 0)
Take dry red kidney beans.

Put them in a bowl.

Add 3× more water than beans.

Leave to soak overnight (8–10 hours).

Next day, drain the water.

Put beans in a pot.

Add fresh water (beans fully covered).

Boil 60–90 minutes until soft.

Drain and set aside.

STEP 2 — COOK THE MAIN STEW (ONE POT)
In a large pot, add:

All cooked kidney beans

Frozen sweet potato

Frozen carrot

Add water just until food is barely covered.

Turn heat to medium.

Cook 25–30 minutes, stirring occasionally.

When sweet potatoes are soft, add:

Salt

Pepper

Cumin powder

Stir well.

Turn off heat.

Let it cool completely.

STEP 3 — COOK GREENS & EDAMAME (SHORT COOK)
In a separate pot, add:

Frozen spinach or kale

Frozen edamame

Add water to cover.

Boil 5–7 minutes.

Drain all water completely.

Let cool.

STEP 4 — DIVIDE INTO 4 MEALS
Take 4 containers.

Divide the bean & sweet potato stew evenly into all 4.

Add greens & edamame evenly into all 4.

Close lids.


Storage

Put 2 containers in fridge.

Put 2 containers in freezer.

STEP 5 — AVOCADO (DO NOT HEAT)
Each day before eating:

Thaw frozen avocado.

Mash it.

Add:

Lemon juice

Salt

Pepper

Mix.

Keep aside.

STEP 6 — REHEATING (CROCK-POT PORTABLE)
Put one container into Crock-Pot.

Turn on LOW.

Heat 60–90 minutes.

Stir once if possible.

Turn off.

Add avocado on top.

Eat.
