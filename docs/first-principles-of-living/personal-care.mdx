## Complete Hair Wash Routine (6 Steps)

### 1. Pre-wet hair
Use lukewarm water
wit filling in hand by wtting on pouring to hair
Wet scalp and hair for 30–60 seconds
Ensure everything is Fully soak scalp and hair

### 2. Apply shampoo (correct amount)
Take coin-sized amount
Rub in hands
Apply ONLY to scalp
Use:
- CeraVe Hydrating Shampoo (main days)
- Nizoral Anti-Dandruff Shampoo (1–2x/week only)

### 3. Gentle scalp massage
Use fingertips (not nails)
Light circular motion
Duration: 30–60 seconds

### 4. Let shampoo sit
Leave on scalp for ~1 minute
Do not scrub during this time

### 5. Rinse thoroughly
Medium pressure water
Let foam run through hair
Rinse with lukewarm water
Duration: 1–2 minutes
Let foam clean the hair lengths naturally

### 6. Towel dry correctly
Use towel to gently press/pat hair
Do NOT rub or twist
Leave hair slightly damp (not fully dry)
Let it air dry there will slight wetness
Ideal state:
- Not dripping
- Slightly damp
- Cool to touch
- No water drops falling

👉 This is called "damp", and it's perfect

## 💧 Aftercare (important for your case)
Apply 1–2 drops jojoba oil to scalp
Light application only

## ⚠️ Key rules
- No hot water
- No aggressive scrubbing
- No daily washing
- Shampoo only scalp

## ✔️ Summary
Total wash time: 5–7 minutes
Result: clean scalp + preserved moisture
