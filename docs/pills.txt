2️⃣ Daily Styling — 60 Seconds Total

Step-by-step

0:00 – 0:10
Towel-dry your hair
→ leave it slightly damp

0:10 – 0:20
(OPTIONAL) 2–3 sprays of sea salt spray
→ run hands through hair

0:20 – 0:40
Take pea-size amount of matte clay/cream
→ warm it fully in your palms
→ apply from back to front
→ then sides & top

0:40 – 0:60
Style:
	•	brush or finger-comb hair back and slightly to the side
	•	keep it soft and natural
	•	do NOT slick it flat

Done.

Optional final mist of light hairspray.
