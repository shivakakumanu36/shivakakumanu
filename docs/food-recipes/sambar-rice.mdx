Sambar rice is a one-dish meal where aromatic lentils and vegetables mingle; this version keeps the quantity right for one person and keeps the gravy thick enough to cling to every grain.

## Ingredients
- ½ cup cooked rice
- ¼ cup mixed vegetables (drumstick, carrot, pumpkin), chopped
- ¼ cup cooked toor dal
- ½ tablespoon tamarind pulp
- ½ tablespoon sambar powder
- Salt to taste
- 1 teaspoon oil
- ¼ teaspoon mustard seeds
- ¼ teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 dried red chili
- A pinch of asafoetida
- 4–5 curry leaves
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

### 1. Build the sambar base
- Simmer the vegetables with tamarind pulp, sambar powder, and a splash of water until the veggies are tender.
- Stir in the cooked dal, adjust the consistency with a little water, and keep it simmering for 5 minutes. Add salt to taste.

### 2. Combine with rice
- Mix the cooked rice into the simmering sambar so every grain is coated. Keep it on low heat for another 2 minutes so the rice absorbs the flavor.

### 3. Temper and serve
- Heat oil in a small tadka pan, add mustard seeds, cumin, chili, asafoetida, and curry leaves until they sizzle.
- Pour the tempering over the sambar rice, garnish with cilantro, and serve hot with papad or pickles.

## Chef’s Notes
- Use slightly undercooked rice so it does not become mushy when combined with the sambar.
- A squeeze of lime or extra curry leaves before serving brightens the aroma.

> **Rice baseline:** I always cook ⅓ cup raw rice (≈67 g), which yields roughly 160 g cooked rice; that’s the number the calories table tracks.
