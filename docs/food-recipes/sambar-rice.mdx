# Sambar Rice

A South Indian one-pot meal where cooked rice meets tangy sambar and aromatic seasoning.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice, cooked
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (drumstick, carrot, pumpkin)
- ½ cup toor dal, cooked
- 1 tablespoon tamarind paste
- 1 tablespoon sambar powder
- Salt to taste
- 1 tablespoon oil
- ½ teaspoon mustard seeds
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 dried red chili
- A pinch of asafoetida
- Curry leaves
- Chopped cilantro for garnish

## Method
1. Cook vegetables until tender, add sambar powder, tamarind, cooked dal, and water.
2. Simmer for 5 minutes, then mix in cooked rice to combine; adjust salt.
3. Heat oil, temper mustard, cumin, curry leaves, chili, and hing; pour over sambar rice.
4. Garnish with cilantro and serve with papad or chutney.
