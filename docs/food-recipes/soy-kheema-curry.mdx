# Soy Kheema Curry

Plant-based kheema with rehydrated soy granules, onions, tomatoes, and warming spices.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup soy granules
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 1 teaspoon red chili powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- ½ teaspoon cumin powder
- ½ teaspoon garam masala
- Salt to taste
- Fresh mint or cilantro for garnish

## Method
1. Rehydrate soy granules in hot water for 10 minutes, squeeze out excess liquid.
2. Heat oil, sauté bay leaf, add onions until golden.
3. Stir in ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes; cook into a thick masala.
4. Add rehydrated soy, the powdered spices, and cook for 5 minutes.
5. Pour in a splash of water, simmer for 6–8 minutes; finish with garam masala and herbs.
