---
title: "Soy Kheema Curry"
description: "Protein-rich soy granules cooked in Indian spices, scaled for one plate."
servings: "1 portion"
---

Plant-based kheema that keeps the soy granules juicy and the spices balanced for a single serving of hearty curry.

## Ingredients
- ½ cup soy granules
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1 bay leaf
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- ½ small onion, finely chopped
- ½ teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- ½ tomato, finely chopped
- ¼ teaspoon red chili powder
- ¼ teaspoon coriander powder
- ⅛ teaspoon cumin powder
- ⅛ teaspoon garam masala
- Salt to taste
- 1 tablespoon chopped mint or cilantro
- ½ cup water (for simmering)

### 1. Rehydrate the soy
- Soak the soy granules in hot water for 10 minutes, then squeeze out excess moisture and set aside.

### 2. Prepare the masala
- Heat oil in a pan, add bay leaf and cumin seeds, then sauté the onion until translucent.
- Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and tomato, cook until the mixture thickens and releases oil.

### 3. Simmer the kheema
- Add the rehydrated soy granules along with the powdered spices and salt, stirring to coat everything evenly.
- Pour in the water, cover, and simmer on low for 6–8 minutes so the soy absorbs the masala; finish with garam masala and herbs.

## Chef’s Notes
- Drain the soy well so it picks up the curry without becoming soupy.
- A splash of lime or chopped mint just before serving adds freshness.
