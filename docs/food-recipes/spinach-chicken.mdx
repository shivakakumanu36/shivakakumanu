This single-serving spinach chicken keeps the gravy silky while letting the spinach’s earthiness mingle with light heat.

## Ingredients
- 200 g chicken pieces (thigh or breast)
- 1 tablespoon oil
- ¼ teaspoon cumin seeds
- ½ small onion, chopped
- ½ teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1 small tomato, chopped
- ¼ teaspoon coriander powder
- ¼ teaspoon garam masala
- ⅛ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ cup spinach puree (blitzed with a splash of water)
- 2 tablespoons yogurt (optional, for creaminess)
- Salt to taste
- Fenugreek leaves (optional)
- ½ cup cooked lentils (moong or toor)

### 1. Build the base
- Heat the oil in a pan, add cumin seeds, and let them crackle.
- Sauté the onions until translucent, then stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook until fragrant.
- Add the tomato, turmeric, coriander, and a pinch of salt; cook until the tomatoes break down.

### 2. Cook the chicken
- Add the chicken pieces to the masala, sear for a few minutes on medium heat.
- Pour in ¼ cup water, cover, and simmer until the chicken is almost cooked through (about 8 minutes).

### 3. Finish with spinach
- Pour in the spinach puree and yogurt (if using), stir well, and simmer uncovered for 3–4 minutes.
- Add garam masala, fenugreek leaves, and adjust salt just before serving.
- Stir in the cooked lentils so they warm through and soak in the spinach gravy before plating.

## Chef’s Notes
- Use fresh spinach for the brightest color; blanch and blend before starting.
- A splash of cream or extra yogurt can mellow the spice if you prefer a richer gravy.
