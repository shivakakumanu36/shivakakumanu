# Spinach Chicken

A protein-rich curry pairing earthy spinach with succulent chicken thighs and subtle heat.

## Ingredients
- 500 g chicken pieces
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- ¼ teaspoon turmeric
- 1 cup spinach puree
- ½ cup yogurt
- Salt to taste
- Fenugreek leaves (optional)

## Method
1. Heat oil, add cumin seeds, and sauté onions until golden.
2. Add ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes; cook until soft.
3. Toss in spices and chicken, sear for 5–6 minutes.
4. Stir in spinach puree and yogurt; simmer until chicken is cooked.
5. Finish with fenugreek leaves and adjust seasoning.
