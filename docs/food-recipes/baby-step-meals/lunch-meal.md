---
title: Lunch Meal
---

Simple, repeatable breakfast ideas to get started.

## Air Fryer Veg Base

- Set to Air Fry mode at 400°F (200°C) for 15 min.
- Add sweet potatoes in a single layer
- When ~8 min remain add carrots.
- When ~4 min remain, add edamame.
- Let everything sit in basket 2-3 min (fryer off).
- Add peanuts, air fry 2-3 min max or raw for crunch.

## Avocado

- If frozen, leave on the counter 30-60 min (or fridge overnight) until soft.
- Smash it lightly with a fork.

## Assemble

- 1/8 tsp each salt, pepper, smoked paprika; optional pinch garlic powder + cumin; finish with a squeeze of lime.
