Fluffy rice interspersed with garden vegetables and warm spices makes this veg pulao a quick yet satisfying solo meal.

## Ingredients
- ¾ cup basmati rice
- 1 teaspoon oil or ghee
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 cloves
- 1 cardamom pod
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- ¼ teaspoon fennel seeds (optional)
- ⅓ small onion, sliced
- ½ teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- ¼ cup carrots, diced
- ¼ cup green beans, chopped
- ¼ cup green peas
- ½ tomato, chopped
- 1 cup water
- Salt to taste
- Fresh mint or cilantro for garnish

### 1. Prep the rice and spices
- Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, soak for 10 minutes, then drain.
- Heat the oil, add the whole spices and cumin, and toast for 30 seconds until fragrant.

### 2. Sauté the vegetables
- Add the onion and ginger-garlic paste, cook until translucent.
- Toss in the carrots, beans, peas, and tomato; sauté for 2 minutes until the vegetables soften slightly.

### 3. Simmer with rice
- Stir in the drained rice so it gets coated in the oil, then pour in water and salt.
- Cover, reduce the heat, and simmer for 12 minutes until the water is absorbed.
- Turn off the flame, let it rest for 4 minutes, then fluff with a fork and garnish with mint or cilantro.

## Chef’s Notes
- Keep the vegetable cuts uniform so they cook together with the rice.
- Use lightly cooked rice for the best texture when the pulao rests.

> **Rice baseline:** I always treat rice as ⅓ cup raw (≈67 g), which cooks to about 160 g cooked; that’s the measurement the calories table tracks.
