# Veg Pulao

Fluffy rice cooked with garden vegetables and warm spices for an easy dinner or lunch.

## Ingredients
- 1½ cups basmati rice
- 2 tablespoons oil or ghee
- 1 bay leaf
- 4 cloves
- 2 cardamom pods
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- ½ cup carrots, diced
- ½ cup green beans, chopped
- ½ cup green peas
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 2 cups water
- Salt to taste
- Mint leaves for garnish

## Method
1. Rinse rice and soak for 15 minutes.
2. Heat oil, add whole spices and cumin until aromatic.
3. Sauté onions and ginger-garlic paste until soft.
4. Add vegetables and cook for 3 minutes.
5. Stir in drained rice, tomatoes, water, and salt.
6. Cover and simmer on low for 15 minutes. Garnish with mint.
