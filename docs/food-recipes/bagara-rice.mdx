# Bagara Rice

A fragrant rice tempered with coconut, peanuts, and aromatic spices, traditionally served with rich gravies.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 1 tablespoon ghee
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- 4-5 cashews, broken
- 2 tablespoons grated coconut
- 1 tablespoon roasted peanuts
- 2 cloves
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 3 cups water
- Salt to taste

## Method
1. Rinse and soak the rice for 20 minutes.
2. In a pan, heat ghee and add whole spices until fragrant.
3. Add coconut, peanuts, and cashews; sauté briefly.
4. Drain the rice, add to the pan, and sauté until the grains are coated.
5. Pour in water, bring to boil, cover, and simmer for 12–15 minutes.
6. Let it rest, then fluff before serving with curry.
