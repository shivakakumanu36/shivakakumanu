---
title: "Bagara Rice"
description: "A Hyderabadi-style bagara rice tempered with spices, coconut, and peanuts for a comforting single-plate meal."
servings: "1 portion"
---

Bagara rice is the perfect companion for rich gravies; this single portion keeps the spice balance delicate so you enjoy every fragrant grain.

## Ingredients
- ½ cup basmati rice
- 1 teaspoon ghee or neutral oil
- ¼ teaspoon cumin seeds
- 2 cashews, broken
- 1 tablespoon grated coconut
- 1 teaspoon roasted peanuts
- 1 clove
- ½ cinnamon stick
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 green cardamom pod
- 1 cup water
- Salt to taste

### 1. Rinse and toast
- Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then soak for 15 minutes. Drain and set aside.
- Warm the ghee in a small saucepan, add the whole spices, and let them sizzle for 30 seconds until fragrant.

### 2. Build the tempering
- Add the cashews, coconut, and peanuts to the pan; sauté lightly until the nuts begin to brown and the coconut becomes aromatic.
- Stir in the drained rice and coat every grain in the spiced fat for about a minute.

### 3. Simmer and rest
- Pour in the water, add salt, and bring the mixture to a gentle boil. Cover, reduce the heat to low, and simmer for 12 minutes.
- Turn off the heat, let it stand covered for another 5 minutes, then fluff the rice with a fork before serving.

## Chef’s Notes
- Toast the nuts and coconut carefully; browned rather than burnt bits give the best texture.
- Use a tight lid so the steam cooks the grains evenly without stirring.

> **Rice baseline:** I always work with ⅓ cup raw rice (≈67 g); that cooks to roughly 160 g, which is the weight used for the calories table.
