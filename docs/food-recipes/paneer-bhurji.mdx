# Paneer Bhurji

Crumbled paneer tossed with sautéed aromatics and spices, ready in under 15 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 200 g paneer, grated or crumbled
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1 green chili, chopped
- 1-inch ginger, grated
- 1 tomato, chopped
- ½ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon red chili powder
- ½ teaspoon garam masala
- Salt to taste
- Chopped cilantro for garnish

## Method
1. Heat oil in a pan, add cumin seeds and let them crackle.
2. Sauté onions, chili, and ginger until translucent.
3. Add tomatoes and spices; cook until the tomatoes break down.
4. Stir in the paneer, mix thoroughly, and cook for 2–3 minutes.
5. Sprinkle garam masala, adjust salt, and garnish with cilantro before serving.
