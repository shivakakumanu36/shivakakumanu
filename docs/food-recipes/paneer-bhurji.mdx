---
title: "Paneer Bhurji"
description: "Quick scrambled paneer cooked with onions, tomatoes, and warming spices for a satisfying single plate."
servings: "1 portion"
---

This paneer bhurji keeps the aromatics bright while ensuring the paneer stays tender; the spice profile is mild enough for a solo breakfast or snack.

## Ingredients
- 100 g paneer, crumbled
- 1 teaspoon oil
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- ½ small onion, finely chopped
- ½ green chili, chopped
- ½ teaspoon grated ginger
- ½ tomato, chopped
- ⅛ teaspoon turmeric powder
- ⅛ teaspoon red chili powder
- ⅛ teaspoon garam masala
- Salt to taste
- Chopped cilantro for garnish

### 1. Warm the spices
- Heat the oil in a small skillet, add cumin seeds, and let them sizzle until aromatic.
- Sauté the onions, green chili, and ginger until the onions turn translucent.

### 2. Build the bhurji
- Add the tomato, turmeric, chili powder, and salt; cook until the tomato softens.
- Stir in the crumbled paneer and garam masala, cook for 2–3 minutes while mixing gently so the paneer absorbs the spices.
- Finish with cilantro and serve warm with toast, rotis, or steamed rice.

## Chef’s Notes
- Crumble the paneer by hand to keep some texture; over-processing makes it mushy.
- A squeeze of lime brightens the dish if the tomatoes are too sweet.
