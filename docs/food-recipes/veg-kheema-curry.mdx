This page will detail the veg kheema curry recipe with cooking notes and accompaniments.


. Wash and prep first


Rinse vegetables thoroughly.


Trim and chop to the size you want when cooking later.


2. Blanch most vegetables before freezing
Blanching (briefly boiling, then ice-cooling) slows enzyme action that causes loss of color, texture, and flavor.
Typical blanching times:


Leafy greens: 2–3 minutes


Carrots (sliced/diced): ~2 minutes


Broccoli/cauliflower florets: 3 minutes


Green beans: 3 minutes


Peppers, onions, herbs: blanching is optional


Skip blanching for:


Onions


Peppers


Fresh herbs


Garlic
(because they freeze well raw)


3. Dry well before freezing
Pat dry to avoid large ice crystals.
4. Fill the molds


Leave a little headspace for expansion.


Cover with the lids to prevent freezer burn.


Lay flat in the freezer.


5. Storage time (quality guideline)


Best quality within 3–6 months


Herbs: 1–3 months
They remain safe longer, but texture declines.


6. Label with date and contents
This is easy to forget once frozen.
7. Thawing & use


Add directly to soups, sautés, stir-fries, or sauces.


For raw-use (like salads), expect a softer texture after thawing.


Food-safety reminder
Keep freezer at 0°F / –18°C or below.
