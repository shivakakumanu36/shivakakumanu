---
title: "Goat Plus Liver Curry"
description: "Rustic goat curry enriched with tender liver pieces for a punchy, single-person meal."
servings: "1 portion"
---

This one-pot curry balances the deeper flavors of goat with the iron-rich liver; the spices stay bold yet calibrated for just one serving.

## Ingredients
- 150 g goat meat, bone-in pieces
- 80 g liver, cleaned and cut into bite-sized cubes
- 1 tablespoon oil
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- ½ teaspoon fennel seeds
- 1 small onion, thinly sliced
- ½ teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1 small tomato, pureed
- ¼ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon red chili powder (adjust to taste)
- ½ teaspoon coriander powder
- ¼ teaspoon garam masala
- Salt to taste
- A few curry leaves (optional)
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- ½ cup water (or as needed during cooking)

### 1. Sear the masala
- Heat oil in a pressure-safe pan, add cumin and fennel; let them pop before adding the onions.
- Sauté until the onions turn golden, then stir in the ginger-garlic paste and fry for a minute.
- Add the tomato puree and powdered spices, cook until the masala releases oil and becomes thick.

### 2. Cook the goat
- Add the goat pieces, season with salt, and sear for 2–3 minutes until slightly browned.
- Pour in ½ cup water, close the lid, and simmer on low (or pressure-cook for 10 minutes) until the goat is tender.

### 3. Finish with liver
- Stir in the liver cubes, cover, and cook for another 6–8 minutes so they are cooked through but still tender.
- Sprinkle garam masala and curry leaves, adjust salt, and simmer uncovered until the gravy slightly thickens.
- Garnish with cilantro before serving with rice or rotis.

## Chef’s Notes
- Do not overcook the liver; it should soften but not become grainy.
- Allow the curry to rest before serving so the aromas settle.
