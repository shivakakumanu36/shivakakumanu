# Goat Plus Liver Curry

Hearty goat pieces simmered with liver, onions, and spices for a rich, protein-forward curry.

## Ingredients
- 500 g goat pieces, bone-in
- 200 g liver, cleaned and diced
- 3 tablespoons oil
- 2 onions, finely sliced
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tomatoes, pureed
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 1½ teaspoons red chili powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- ½ teaspoon garam masala
- Salt to taste
- Fresh curry leaves

## Method
1. Heat oil, sauté onions until deep golden, add ginger-garlic paste.
2. Add tomato puree, spices, and cook until the masala leaves oil.
3. Add goat pieces, sear briefly, add water, cover, and pressure cook until tender.
4. Stir in liver cubes, simmer 6–8 minutes more so they are cooked but tender.
5. Finish with curry leaves, adjust salt, and serve hot with rice or paratha.
