This coriander pulao keeps the herbs vibrant; the single portion is perfect for a solo meal or a light lunch when you want something aromatic yet light.

## Ingredients
- ½ cup basmati rice
- 1 teaspoon oil or ghee
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 cloves
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- ¼ teaspoon fennel seeds (optional)
- ½ small onion, thinly sliced
- ½ teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1 green chili, slit lengthwise
- ¼ cup diced carrots
- ¼ cup green peas
- 1 small tomato, chopped
- ½ cup fresh coriander leaves, roughly chopped
- ¾ cup water
- Salt to taste

### 1. Toast the whole spices
- Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then soak it for 15 minutes and keep covered.
- Heat the oil in a pan, add bay leaf, cloves, cinnamon, cumin, and fennel. Let them sizzle for a few seconds until fragrant.

### 2. Build the masala
- Add the onions and sauté until translucent, then stir in the ginger-garlic paste and green chili.
- Toss in the carrots, peas, and tomato; cook for 2–3 minutes so the vegetables soften slightly.

### 3. Finish the pulao
- Drain the rice and add it to the pan, stirring gently to coat the grains with the masala.
- Pour in the water, add salt, cover, and simmer on low for 12 minutes.
- Once done, fluff with a fork, sprinkle over the chopped coriander, and serve hot.

## Chef’s Notes
- Use fresh coriander leaves for the final toss to keep the herbaceous aroma bright.
- A squeeze of lime just before serving wakes up the spices without overpowering them.

> **Rice baseline:** I always work from ⅓ cup raw rice (≈67 g), which cooks to about 160 g—this is the baseline referenced in the calories table.
