# Burnt Garlic Jeera Rice

Golden basmati rice tossed with browned garlic and cumin seeds for a nutty, smoky aroma that pairs well with any curry.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup basmati rice, rinsed until the water runs clear
- 2 tablespoons ghee or neutral oil
- 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 green chili, slit
- 2 cups water
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

## Method
1. Soak the rice for 20 minutes, then drain.
2. Heat ghee in a pan, add cumin seeds and let them sizzle.
3. Add sliced garlic and fry until deeply golden but not burnt—this is what gives the dish its character.
4. Toss in the green chili, drained rice, and salt; stir for another minute to coat the grains.
5. Add water, bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low for 15 minutes.
6. Remove from heat, let rest for 5 minutes, fluff with a fork, and garnish with cilantro.
