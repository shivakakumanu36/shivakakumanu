---

Smooth tomato sauce and tender meatballs over al dente spaghetti make a comforting solo dinner that comes together quickly.

## Ingredients
- 100 g spaghetti
- 150 g ground meat (beef, pork, or a mix)
- ¼ small onion, grated or finely chopped
- 1 egg yolk
- 2 tablespoons breadcrumbs
- ⅛ teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 cup marinara or tomato sauce
- Grated Parmesan and basil (optional)
- Salt and black pepper to taste

### 1. Shape the meatballs
- Combine the ground meat with onion, egg yolk, breadcrumbs, oregano, salt, and pepper.
- Roll into 6–8 small, bite-sized meatballs, keeping them uniform so they cook evenly.

### 2. Cook the meatballs
- Heat half the olive oil in a pan and sear the meatballs on all sides until browned and cooked through; transfer them to a plate.

### 3. Finish in sauce
- Add the remaining oil to the pan, warm the marinara sauce, and slide the meatballs back in.
- Simmer uncovered for 5 minutes so the sauce clings to the meatballs while you cook the spaghetti.

### 4. Plate it up
- Cook the spaghetti according to package directions, drain, and toss briefly with the sauced meatballs.
- Serve hot with a sprinkle of Parmesan and basil if you like.

## Chef’s Notes
- Keep the meatballs compact but not overworked so they stay tender.
- Reserve a little pasta water to loosen the sauce if it stiffens while plating.
