# Spaghetti Meat Ball Pasta

Smooth tomato sauce and tender meatballs over al dente spaghetti make a comforting dinner.

## Ingredients
- 250 g spaghetti
- 300 g ground meat (beef, pork, or mixed)
- 1 small onion, grated
- 1 egg
- ¼ cup breadcrumbs
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 cups marinara sauce
- Parmesan and basil for garnish

## Method
1. Mix meat, onion, egg, breadcrumbs, and oregano; shape into small balls.
2. Pan-fry meatballs until browned all over and cooked through; set aside.
3. Heat marinara sauce, gently add meatballs and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Cook spaghetti according to package, drain, and toss with sauce if desired.
5. Serve meatballs over spaghetti, finish with Parmesan and basil.
