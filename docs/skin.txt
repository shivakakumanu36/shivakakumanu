⭐ My Honest Recommendation (Ranked)

🥇 #1 — Professional Chemical Peels (Best balance of speed + safety)

→ This is what I would choose for you first

Why?

✔ Very effective for brown post-acne marks
✔ Evens skin tone
✔ Lower risk than lasers on darker skin
✔ Results within 2–6 weeks
✔ Affordable compared to laser

Best peel types for your skin tone

• Mandelic Acid Peel (most gentle + brightening)
• Lactic Acid Peel (hydrating + brightening)
• Light Glycolic Peel (10–30%)

👉 Avoid very strong TCA peels at first

Typical plan

• 3–4 sessions
• 3–4 weeks apart

Expected improvement

• 20–30% after 1 session
• 50–70% after 3 sessions
• more even tone + glow

Downtime

• Mild peeling 2–4 days

⸻

🥈 #2 — Laser Toning (Q-Switched / Pico Laser)

→ Choose ONLY with an experienced dermatologist

Pros

✔ Faster fading than peels for some people
✔ Great for forehead patches
✔ Little peeling

Risks for darker skin (important)

⚠️ If done wrong, can darken skin more

So:
✔ choose dermatologist, NOT salon
✔ make sure they treat brown skin frequently

Sessions

• 2–4 sessions usually

Results

• visible fading within 2–6 weeks
