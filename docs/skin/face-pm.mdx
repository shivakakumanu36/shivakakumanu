# Face PM Routine

Wind down with a deeper treatment that supports repair and recovery. Remove makeup thoroughly before applying richer products designed to soothe overnight.

- Double cleanse: Start with an oil or balm cleanser, then follow with a gentle water-based wash.
- Serum: Layer in a repair-focused serum (retinol, peptides, or antioxidants depending on your goals).
- Moisturizer: Use a richer cream to lock in hydration.
- Optional: Apply an occlusive if your skin is very dry.
