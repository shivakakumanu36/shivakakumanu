# Face AM Routine

Start the day with a gentle cleanse followed by a lightweight moisturizer and SPF protection. Focus on hydration and barrier support, especially if you plan to be outdoors or under screens for extended periods.

- Cleanser: Use a sulfate-free gel or creamy cleanser.
- Toner (optional): Pick one with soothing ingredients like aloe or centella.
- Moisturizer: Choose a non-greasy, fast-absorbing formula.
- Sunscreen: Finish strongly with a broad-spectrum SPF 30+.
